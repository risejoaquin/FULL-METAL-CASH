# SolidPOS LGA-04 Package

This package adds LGA-04 — Limited GA Public GA Decision Readiness or Capacity Remediation.

## Added files
- scripts/ga/validate-lga-04-public-ga-decision-readiness-capacity-remediation.ps1
- scripts/ga/lga-04-public-ga-decision-readiness-capacity-remediation-check.sql
- docs/ga/lga-04-public-ga-decision-readiness-capacity-remediation.md
- docs/ga/lga-04-capacity-remediation-plan.md
- docs/ga/lga-04-public-ga-go-no-go.md
- docs/ga/lga-04-evidence-matrix.md

## Decision
LGA-04 does not activate Public GA. It either keeps Limited GA with capacity remediation, or prepares the next manual Public GA decision package.
