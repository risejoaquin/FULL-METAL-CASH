# SolidPOS Pilot Daily Closing Checklist

Use this checklist after each pilot day.

## Closing validation

- Confirm all pilot sales are visible in sales read model.
- Confirm all expected receipts were generated.
- Confirm returns/refunds are documented, even if count is zero.
- Confirm cash movement notes are complete.
- Confirm expected cash was compared with counted cash.
- Confirm dashboard reports are reachable.
- Confirm audit events are visible.
- Confirm sync status is healthy.
- Confirm no unexpected dead-letter condition was observed.
- Confirm incidents are logged.
- Confirm rollback was not required, or document if it was used.
