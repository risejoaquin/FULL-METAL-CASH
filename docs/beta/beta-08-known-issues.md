# BETA-08 Known Issues

Known issues must include owner, severity and exit criteria before wider expansion.

Current known operational conditions are inherited from monitoring and support evidence, not silently discarded. A known issue may remain non-blocking only when it has an owner and an explicit exit path.

| Issue | Severity | Owner | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| retry/dead-letter operational triage, when present | SEV3 | support | triaged or disposition documented |
| negative inventory reconciliation, when present | SEV3 | operations | reconciled or documented before broader rollout |
| open cash shift daily review, when present | SEV3 | store operations | operator review completed |
