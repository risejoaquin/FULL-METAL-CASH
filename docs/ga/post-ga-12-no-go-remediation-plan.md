# Post-GA-12 No-Go Remediation Plan

`NO_GO_REMEDIATION` is selected when launch expansion should stop until blockers or unacceptable conditions are fixed.

## Required terms

```text
NO_GO_REMEDIATION
blockers
remediation
```

## Remediation workstreams

- Fix any blocker detected by the decision validator.
- Resolve or formally accept capacity conditions.
- Resolve or formally accept DB waiting connection observations.
- Rerun GA-12 and Post-GA-12 after remediation.
