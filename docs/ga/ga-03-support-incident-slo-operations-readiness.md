# GA-03 — Support, Incident and SLO Operations Readiness

## Objective

Convert the support practices proven during pilot/beta into an explicit, sustainable GA operating contract before financial/data reconciliation and stable release-candidate gates.

## Scope

- SEV1–SEV4;
- incident intake and evidence;
- escalation policy and on-call role ownership;
- domain runbooks for sync, cash, payments, inventory, platform, security and release;
- rollback authority;
- SLOs, SLIs and error budget;
- daily support operating checklist;
- post-incident review template;
- current production signal cross-check;
- fresh GA-02 prerequisite.

GA-03 does not create a load test, alerting platform or GA rollout. Those are later readiness gates.
