# LGA-06 Operational Continuity Checklist

## Limited GA Continuity

- Limited GA remains active.
- Public GA not activated.
- Support/on-call readiness remains in place.
- Operational continuity is measured through API, DB, sync, inventory, and financial integrity checks.

## Support Checklist

- Monitor sync pending, retry, conflict, and dead letter counts.
- Monitor inventory negative stock count.
- Monitor open cash shifts.
- Monitor waiting connections and long-running queries.
- Preserve the formal capacity risk accepted decision until Railway Pro / scaling is completed.

## Capacity Risk

Capacity risk is accepted only for Limited GA. It is not permission for Public GA.
