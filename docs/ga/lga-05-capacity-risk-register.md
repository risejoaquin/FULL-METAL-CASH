# LGA-05 Capacity Risk Register

## capacity risk
The capacity boundary probe may remain above the Public GA p95 target until Railway Pro or equivalent scaling is purchased.

## formal acceptance
This risk is formally accepted only for Limited GA. It does not authorize Public GA.

## railway pro
Railway Pro or service scaling remains the planned remediation path.

## limited ga controls
- Max stores remain constrained.
- Max concurrent terminals remain constrained.
- Public GA remains not activated.
- Capacity probe values are recorded as evidence.
