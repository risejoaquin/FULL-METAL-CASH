# EXP-08 — Daily Support Triage Checklist

Run daily triage before expansion/release work.

Checklist:

- health/live is alive
- health/ready is ready
- database ready
- retry_pending reviewed
- retry due reviewed
- dead_letter reviewed
- support ticket exists for known dead-letter
- pending conflicts reviewed
- open cash shift reviewed
- failed payments reviewed
- cash differences reviewed
- audit events present
- latest manifest archived

A support ticket is required when retry_due, dead_letter, failed payment, cash difference, or unresolved conflict requires action.
