# SolidPOS Final Public GA Production Closure

Formal final gate for SolidPOS v1 QSR current scope.

PASS means:
- Public GA remains active.
- Current production baseline is closed at 100% for the defined v1 QSR scope.
- No launch blocker remains in this closure gate.
- Work transitions from product-completion roadmap to normal software lifecycle operations and future evolution.

A PASS does not mean the software will never change. Maintenance, monitoring, security upgrades, incident handling, capacity planning, dependency updates, migrations and future product capabilities continue as normal lifecycle work.
